MIRANDA

Interactive Artist · Est. 18/10/1995 · Amsterdam

N

iccolò Miranda is a digital art director and creative developer who treats the browser like a printing press. His work borrows the muscle of 19th-century woodblock posters — colliding serifs, heavy ink, one hand-stamped seal of color — and rebuilds it in WebGL, motion and type that refuses to behave. Previously at Studio Luchthaven and AVRO&KO; now independent, taking on select collaborations worldwide.

Creative developer based in Amsterdam, NL.
